Privacy Policy

How Trovo Tech Solutions handles information for our clients, their customers and visitors to our website. Last updated: 7 October 2026.

Trovo Tech Solutions ("Trovo", "we", "us") builds websites, web and mobile apps, and business software for businesses across industries, for example billing and invoicing, order, job and service management, staff management and customer notifications. Some of our products send WhatsApp notifications through the WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API) provided by Meta.

This policy explains what information we handle, why, and the choices available to you.

1. Who this policy covers

  • Our clients: the businesses that subscribe to our software, and their staff who use it.
  • Our clients' customers: people whose details a client records, for example to issue an invoice and send it on WhatsApp.
  • Visitors to our website, trovotechsolutions.in.

When our clients use our software to manage their customers, the client decides what customer information is recorded and why. Trovo processes that information on the client's behalf, to provide the service. For our clients' customers, the client is the data fiduciary under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and Trovo is its data processor. For information about our clients, their staff users and visitors to our website, Trovo is the data fiduciary.

3. How we use WhatsApp and Meta data

Our software connects to the WhatsApp Business Platform only with the client's permission, given when the client connects their own WhatsApp Business Account to our app ("Trovo Business Messaging") through Meta's sign-up process, or when the client enters their own access details in our software.

We use this access only to:

  1. Send transactional messages from the client's own WhatsApp number to the client's customers about their transactions with the client, for example an invoice notification with the invoice PDF and amount due when an invoice is generated, and a payment confirmation when an invoice is fully paid.
  2. Create and manage the client's message templates for those notifications, and show their approval status.
  3. Record delivery status (sent, delivered, read, failed) so the client can see whether a message reached the customer.
  4. Monitor the health of the connection (for example, an expired access token or a rejected template) so we can help the client fix it.

We do not send marketing messages, and we do not use WhatsApp data for any other purpose. Messages are sent only to customers the client has recorded, and the client is responsible for obtaining their customers' agreement to receive WhatsApp messages.

4. Legal basis

  • Clients and their staff users, and website visitors: Trovo is the data fiduciary. We process this information based on your consent, and where you have voluntarily provided it to us for a specified purpose (for example to create your account, provide the service you subscribed to, or answer your enquiry).
  • Clients' customers: the client is the data fiduciary, and Trovo processes this information on the client's behalf and on its instructions, as its data processor. The client is responsible for having a lawful basis, such as the customer's consent, including for sending WhatsApp messages.

5. Sharing

We share information only:

  • with Meta Platforms, to deliver WhatsApp messages from the client's own number. Meta's own terms and privacy policy apply to WhatsApp, and Meta may process data outside India;
  • with our hosting and infrastructure providers in India, who store data for us under confidentiality obligations;
  • when required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our clients, users or others.

6. Security

  • Access tokens and other secrets are encrypted (AES-256) and never shown in the software or in our logs.
  • Access to client data is limited by role and permission, and important actions are recorded in an audit log.
  • Data is transmitted over encrypted connections.

8. Your rights and choices

  • Clients can export their data, switch WhatsApp off at any time in our software, disconnect it completely by removing our app in Meta Business Settings, and ask us to delete their account and data.
  • Clients' customers should contact the business that serves them. The business can stop WhatsApp messages to a customer and correct or delete their details. You may also contact us, and we will pass your request to the business.
  • Your rights under the DPDP Act: you may ask us for a summary of the personal data we process about you, ask for it to be corrected, completed or erased, withdraw your consent, and nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf. Contact the grievance officer below. If we do not resolve your grievance, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
  • Data deletion: see https://trovotechsolutions.in/data-deletion.

9. Children

Our software is for businesses and is not directed at children.
