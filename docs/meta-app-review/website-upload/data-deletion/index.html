Data Deletion Instructions

Trovo Tech Solutions — Trovo Business Messaging. How to disconnect WhatsApp and ask us to delete your data.

Our app connects a business's own WhatsApp Business Account to Trovo's business software so that transactional notifications, such as invoices and payment confirmations, can be sent from the business's own number. You can remove this connection and ask us to delete related data at any time.

Disconnect WhatsApp from Trovo

Do either of these:

  1. In the Trovo software, go to Settings → WhatsApp and switch off "Enable WhatsApp Business Integration". Messages stop immediately. The stored access token is deleted when you ask us to delete your data (below), and becomes unusable when you remove our app in Meta (option 2); or
  2. In Meta Business Settings → Integrations / Connected apps (or WhatsApp accounts → Partners), remove Trovo Business Messaging.

After switching off or disconnecting, our software no longer sends messages from your number. Removing our app in Meta (option 2) revokes Trovo's access completely.

Ask us to delete your data

Email privacy@trovotechsolutions.in from the email address registered with your Trovo account, with the subject "Data deletion request", and include your business name and WhatsApp Business Account ID if you have it.

What we delete:

  • the stored WhatsApp access token
  • the WhatsApp account and phone-number identifiers
  • WhatsApp message history
  • on request, your business account and all business data held by Trovo

Before deletion: we confirm the request with you, and you can export your business data first. Records we must keep by law (for example our own tax invoices to you) are kept only as long as required.

Timing: we confirm receipt within 7 days and complete deletion within 30 days.

Customers of a business that uses Trovo: please contact that business, or email us and we will forward your request.
