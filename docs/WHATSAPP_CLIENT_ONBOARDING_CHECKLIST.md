# WhatsApp Invoice Messages — Setup Checklist for Clients

*From Trovo Tech Solutions*

Your Car Spa software can automatically send your customers a WhatsApp message:
1. **when an invoice is generated:** the invoice PDF with the amount due;
2. **when the invoice is fully paid:** a payment-received thank-you.

Messages are sent from **your own WhatsApp business number**, with your business name, and your customers call you directly for any questions. Trovo sets everything up with you in one short session of about 20–30 minutes.

---

## Before the session, please prepare

| # | What | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Your own Facebook account** | Your real personal account; Meta requires one to manage a business. **Don't create a new or fake one.** Trovo never asks for your Facebook password. |
| 2 | **A phone number for business WhatsApp** | A mobile or landline number that is **not currently used on the WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business app**. It must be able to receive an SMS or call for a verification code during the session. If you want to use a number that's already on WhatsApp, delete that WhatsApp account first; ask us. |
| 3 | **Your business display name** | The name customers will see, e.g. *E6 Car Spa – Salem*. Meta reviews it. Franchisees using a brand name need the brand owner's permission. |
| 4 | **A debit or credit card** | Meta charges you directly for messages, about ₹0.12–0.15 per message (check Meta's current rate card). A busy outlet with 300 invoices a month sends about 600 messages, roughly ₹80–90 a month. |
| 5 | **Business phone number for customers** | Shown in every message: "For any questions, please call us on …". Make sure it's correct in **Settings → Company Settings**. |
| 6 | *(Optional)* **GST or registration certificate** | Only needed later if you want Meta business verification for higher sending limits. Not needed to start. |
| 7 | **The computer with your Car Spa software**, signed in as the Owner | |

---

## During the session (Trovo guides you)

1. **Create your Meta business account** at business.facebook.com with your business name and address. Skip this if you already have one.
2. **Add your WhatsApp number** and verify it with the code Meta sends.
3. **Set your display name**, e.g. *E6 Car Spa – Salem*.
4. **Connect WhatsApp to your Car Spa software.**
   - **Current version:** we create a secure access key in **your** Meta business settings together, and you enter it in **Settings → WhatsApp** on your computer. Trovo does not keep a copy.
   - **Cloud version (coming):** you just click **"Connect WhatsApp"** and log in to Meta.
5. **Create the message templates:** one click on **"Create standard templates"**. Meta usually approves them within minutes, at most within a day.
6. **Add your card** in WhatsApp Manager for message charges.
7. **Test:** we send a test invoice message to your own phone.

After Meta approves the templates, click **"Use approved templates"** in Settings → WhatsApp, or Trovo does it with you. From then on, messages are sent automatically.

---

## After setup: what you need to do

| Task | When |
|---|---|
| Ask customers if they want WhatsApp updates, and tick **"Send WhatsApp updates"** on their customer record | When adding or updating a customer |
| Keep your business phone correct in Company Settings | If it changes |
| Keep your card in Meta valid | Always; messages stop if Meta can't charge |
| Contact Trovo if Settings → WhatsApp shows a warning | As needed |

**You don't need to:** design templates, open Meta's template screens, or reply to messages. Customers call you.

## Frequently asked questions

**Who owns the WhatsApp number?**
You do. It's in your own Meta business account. If you stop using our software, the number stays yours.

**Can I change the message wording?**
Yes. Tell Trovo the wording you'd like. We'll check it meets WhatsApp's rules (it must be a receipt or notification, not an advertisement) and set it up for you. Meta approves it before use.

**Will customers see my name?**
Yes. Messages come from your WhatsApp business number and display name, and include your phone number.

**What if a customer replies?**
Replies arrive in your WhatsApp Business account, but the software doesn't manage conversations. The message asks customers to call you instead.

**Does Trovo see my customers' messages?**
No. The software only sends the two automatic messages and records whether they were delivered.
